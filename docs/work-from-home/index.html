The invisible commute

Home is one place.
Your day is not.

At home, every transition happens on the same screen. Stand-up becomes deep work, deep work becomes admin, and somehow a lunch break becomes twelve mystery tabs.

Floppy gives each mode a front door. Open the right context in one move, then clear it away before the next one begins.
