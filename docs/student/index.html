The ready-start advantage 01

Your study session shouldn’t start with an empty workspace.

A course can spread across one useful link, a carefully organized folder, and the one tool that actually works.

A Floppy disk gives those pieces a home. Package the setup for a course, club, or study project, then reopen it whenever you need to get back into the material—without rebuilding the workspace from scratch.

Keep it intentional 03

Your study setup, with good boundaries.

Floppy organizes the context you can already access. It does not unlock restricted course materials or change permissions on files, folders, or sites.

  • ✓ Your own study guides and templates
  • ✓ Public or course-approved links
  • ✓ Files and folders you’re allowed to access
