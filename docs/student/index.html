The shared advantage 01

Nobody should have to start from an empty folder.

Someone in your cohort has already found the useful link. Someone else has the cleanest folder structure. Another person knows which tool actually works.

A Floppy disk gives those pieces a home. Package the setup for a course, club, or study group, then share it so everyone can spend more energy understanding the material—and less energy rebuilding the same workspace alone.

How it helps 02

A tiny system for collective brainpower.

Your group already shares knowledge. Floppy helps give the useful parts a shape you can come back to.

Your knowledge, with good boundaries.

Floppy is for organizing context—not ignoring course rules. Share materials you created, resources you have permission to distribute, and links your group is allowed to access.
